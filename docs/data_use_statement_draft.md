## Data Use & Protection Statement

These dashboards report quality-control testing of drug checking test strips (fentanyl, xylazine and other targets), carried out by the Lieberman Lab at the University of Notre Dame with the lab's registered lot testers. They exist so that harm reduction programs, public health agencies and researchers can see how specific brands and lots of test strips perform. The results describe the strips that were tested, not any person or drug sample. The data, results, photographs and text displayed here are protected under applicable copyright, data protection and intellectual property laws [confirm wording with Notre Dame's Office of Technology Commercialization / counsel].

### What the data are and where they come from
Results are submitted by password-protected lab testers through the lot checking data collector form. Each submission records the strip brand, lot number and expiration date, the tester's name and affiliation, the readings for the target and for interfering substances, optional notes, and photographs of the packaging and strips. [Tester names and affiliations appear in the submission log on these pages. Confirm this is intended, or remove them from the published log.] Photographs have their location and device metadata removed before they are published.

### Permitted uses
Anyone may view these dashboards, link to them, and cite them. Harm reduction programs, health departments and other public health organizations may use the lot-level results to decide which test strips to buy, distribute or recommend, and may quote individual lot results in their own materials with attribution: "Lieberman Lab, University of Notre Dame, Lot Checking Dashboard, [URL], accessed [date]." Programs or researchers who want the underlying lot-level dataset may request it by contacting [Prof. Lieberman, mlieberm@nd.edu].

### Prohibited uses
Unauthorized scraping, crawling, harvesting, indexing, or automated collection of any data or photographs from this site is prohibited. This applies to all third parties, including but not limited to artificial intelligence systems, large language model training pipelines, commercial data aggregators, and any automated bots or agents. No data or photographs from this site may be reproduced in bulk, redistributed, sold, sublicensed, incorporated into AI training datasets, or republished in other dashboards or databases without prior written authorization from the Lieberman Lab. [Optional: results may not be used in advertising or marketing of any test strip product.]

### Accuracy and limits of the results
Results reflect the specific lots, concentrations, matrices and replicates tested in the lab, and may not predict how another lot of the same brand, or the same strip in field conditions, will perform. The dashboards are an aid to purchasing and quality-control decisions and are not a substitute for a manufacturer's instructions for use. Corrections are welcome at [mlieberm@nd.edu].

### Website analytics
These pages use GoatCounter to count page views and which view of a dashboard is opened. It sets no cookies, stores no persistent visitor identifier and does no cross-site tracking.

### Enforcement
We reserve the right to block any entity engaging in unauthorized data collection and to pursue other remedies available to us. By accessing this site, you agree to these terms.

## Notes on what I changed from the UNC model
- UNC's audience is individual clients and registered programs who receive results by sample ID; here the data describe commercial products and are meant to be public, so "Permitted uses" is broader and the restriction targets bulk reuse rather than viewing.
- Added: data sources and what is collected, a short accuracy disclaimer, an analytics line, and photo metadata removal. Dropped: sample ID / QR code access.
