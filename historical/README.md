# Historical data

Reference results that did not come through a response form and are not rebuilt weekly.

- `mts_hrj_2025.json`: medetomidine (MTS) lots and 2 mg/mL interference results published in Amate and Lieberman, Harm Reduction Journal 2025 (doi 10.1186/s12954-025-01387-6), transcribed from the article's Tables 1 and 2 and Figure 4. Meant to be merged into the medetomidine dashboard as "published study" lots once `build_mts.py` exists (see the wiki page Medetomidine-Dashboard-Plan). The file's `_source`, `scoring` and `caveats` fields say what was and was not transcribed.
